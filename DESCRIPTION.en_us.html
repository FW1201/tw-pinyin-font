Taiwan Pinyin Kai (臺灣拼音楷) is a Traditional Chinese typeface that displays Hanyu Pinyin above every character, following the pronunciation standard of Taiwan's Ministry of Education. It is based on LXGW WenKai TC.

Polyphonic characters are resolved automatically by about 1,500 contextual word rules, and any reading can be pinned in plain text with ideographic variation selectors (U+E01E0 + n), so the chosen reading survives copy and paste. Reading data comes from the MOE Mandarin Polyphone Review Table and CNS11643.

To contribute, see github.com/FW1201/tw-pinyin-font.
